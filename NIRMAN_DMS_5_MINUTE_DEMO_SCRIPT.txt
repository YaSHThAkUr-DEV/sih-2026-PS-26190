[0:35 - 1:15] SCENE 2: THE DOCUMENT VAULT & ENVELOPE ENCRYPTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SCREEN:  Click 'Documents' on the sidebar.
ACTIONS: Filter by 'T5 Top Secret', click on any document to expand its details
         drawer, then click the 'Verify Hash' button to show the green check.

SPOKEN SCRIPT:
"Let’s jump into our Document Registry.

Every file uploaded here is automatically protected with military-grade
encryption (AES-256). Even if someone hacked the underlying server, they would
only see unreadable scrambled data.

We also have 5 strict clearance levels — from Public (T1) all the way up to
Top Secret (T5). An officer only sees what their rank permits.

And if you click 'Verify Hash', our system checks the file's digital fingerprint
in real-time to mathematically prove that nobody has altered a single letter
since it was saved."


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[1:15 - 1:50] SCENE 3: SMART SEARCH & DEEP OCR
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SCREEN:  Click 'Search & OCR' on the sidebar.
ACTIONS: Type 'Section 65B' or 'Directive' into the OCR search box.
         Click a search result card to show the extracted text snippet and tags.

SPOKEN SCRIPT:
"Government files often contain hundreds of scanned pages, FIRs, or legal
notices. Finding the right document used to take hours.

With our Deep OCR Engine, our system reads and indexes every scanned PDF and
image automatically.

I can simply search for a court case number, a name, or a legal provision like
'Section 65B', and within a fraction of a second, it highlights the exact
paragraph across thousands of documents."


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[1:50 - 2:30] SCENE 4: MAKER-CHECKER (DUAL-CUSTODY APPROVALS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SCREEN:  Click 'Sensitive Approvals' on the sidebar.
ACTIONS: Click on a pending review item in the queue, show the requester info,
         click 'Sanction & Promote' / 'Approve', enter a quick comment, and watch
         the live digital signing complete.

SPOKEN SCRIPT:
"In high-security environments, a single officer should never be able to
silently change an official docket. We enforce a strict 'Maker-Checker' rule.

When an officer submits a revision or a new sensitive file, it sits in this
queue.

A designated senior approver reviews the changes, checks the justification, and
digitally signs off. Only then does the new version go live, while the original
version remains permanently preserved in history."


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[2:30 - 3:15] SCENE 5: WORM RETENTION & THE DISPOSAL COCKPIT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SCREEN:  Click 'Retention Holds' on the sidebar.
ACTIONS: Show the 5 statutory schedules at the top. Filter by 'Active Locks Only'
         to show the frozen WORM records. Then click a record staged for
         disposal and point to the Disposal Cockpit on the right.

SPOKEN SCRIPT:
"Now let’s look at Retention and Legal Holds.

By law, certain records must be kept for 7 years, 30 years, or permanently. We
follow the WORM rule — Write Once, Read Many — meaning nobody can delete locked
records.

If a court places an evidentiary hold on a case, we flip a switch to freeze the
docket completely.

And when a standard document finally reaches the end of its life, our Disposal
Cockpit requires two separate authorized officers (Key 1 and Key 2) to sign off
before the file is safely shredded from the server."


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[3:15 - 3:55] SCENE 6: BLOCKCHAIN PROOF & AUDIT 360
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SCREEN:  Click 'Blockchain Ledger', then click 'Audit Trail'.
ACTIONS: Show confirmed Fabric blocks on the blockchain. In Audit Trail, click
         an event row to open the Officer Dossier popup with IP telemetry.

SPOKEN SCRIPT:
"To ensure evidence can stand up in a court of law, we anchor every critical
action to a Hyperledger Fabric Blockchain.

Over in Auditor 360, compliance teams have a tamper-proof event timeline. Every
login, file download, or decryption request is tracked with exact timestamps, IP
addresses, and officer credentials.

If anyone ever tries to access something they shouldn't, it is instantly
flagged for internal vigilance."


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[3:55 - 4:40] SCENE 7: THE INTER-AGENCY SOVEREIGN HIGHWAY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SCREEN:  Click 'Inter-Agency Highway' on the sidebar.
ACTIONS: Point out the 6 connected agencies (High Court, CBI, Police, FSL Labs,
         Revenue, Digital Authority). Click 'Active Requisitions' and show an
         active shared document with dynamic watermarking.

SPOKEN SCRIPT:
"One of our proudest innovations is the Inter-Agency Sovereign Highway.

Currently, if the Police or High Court need a forensic report from the Cyber
Labs or CBI, files are often emailed or moved on USB pen drives — which is a
major security risk.

With our highway, agencies request files directly through secure channels with
strict time limits. Once approved, the recipient gets a view-only link with an
invisible watermark that automatically expires in a few days. The chain of
custody is 100% maintained."
